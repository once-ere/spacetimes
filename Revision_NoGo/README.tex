\hypertarget{what-this-folder-is}{%
\section{1. What this folder is}\label{what-this-folder-is}}

The theorems of McGlinn, O'Raifeartaigh and Coleman and Mandula forbid a
nontrivial union of the Lorentz symmetry of space-time with an internal
symmetry; their graded extension (Haag, Łopuszański and Sohnius) allows
exactly one way around them, supersymmetry. A proved theorem has no
counterexample while its hypotheses hold, so this folder does the one
thing that can be done: on the octonionic spacetime of signature \(7+1\)
and the split-octonionic spacetime of signature \(4+4\) it constructs,
for each hypothesis of the theorems, an explicit structure that violates
it and in which Lorentz and internal symmetries are combined, and it
verifies every statement in exact arithmetic. The constructions use the
non-associativity of the octonions (octonionic charges have no operator
representation), Cartan's triality (present for \(4+4\), provably absent
for \(7+1\)), the octonionic locking of space rotations to internal
rotations by \(\mathrm G_2\), an exchange of space-time and internal
directions by a split-octonion unit, the super-Poincaré algebras of both
spacetimes with R-symmetry and tensorial charges, and \textbf{combined
manifolds}: a four-dimensional world \(\mathcal M^{3,1}_{\rm NG}\)
(coordinates \(x1,x2,x3,x4\)) times a four-dimensional fibre
\(\mathcal K_{\rm NG}\) (coordinates \(x0,x5,x6,x7\)), on which six
generators of the eight-dimensional Lorentz group become an internal
symmetry.

On the combined manifolds a single 16-component Dirac field, which has
no Lorentz-scalar pair in eight dimensions, forms spin-zero pairs that
are vectors and two-forms of the fibre. The folder analyses their
superfluid: the Bogoliubov-de Gennes spectrum, the effective potential,
the vacuum threshold, the Cooper instability, the competition with
spin-one pairing, the amplitude mode at twice the gap, and the patterns
of symmetry breaking, one of which locks a rotation of the
eight-dimensional Lorentz group to the fermion number. It then answers
the question whether the combined manifolds cure the
non-renormalizability of the eight-dimensional theory: \textbf{they do
not} --- a geometric fibre restores the eight-dimensional divergence
above its inverse radius (and on \(4+4\) brings tachyons); an internal
fibre gives a four-dimensional theory, renormalizable in its Yukawa
form, in which eight-dimensional spacetime has been given up. Every
statement is checked by machine: 94 checks of the program, all passing.

\hypertarget{how-to-run-everything}{%
\section{4. How to run everything}\label{how-to-run-everything}}

From this folder:

\begin{verbatim}
# all data files and the checks of the program (about one second):
cargo run --release --manifest-path code/nogo-rs/Cargo.toml -- all
# the thirteen figures:
python3 code/scripts/make_figures.py
# everything, in order, with one verdict at the end ("ALL PASS"):
bash code/scripts/check_all.sh
\end{verbatim}

Requirements: a Rust toolchain for the edition 2021; Python 3 with NumPy
and Matplotlib; for the documents TeX Live with REVTeX 4.2, XeLaTeX and
latexmk, the DejaVu fonts and the Latin Modern Math font, and pandoc.
The program uses the library crate \texttt{code/spacetimes-rs} of the
repository (for the Dirac matrices \(\gamma^A_{1671}\) and
\(\gamma^A_{1644}\) of the project and for numerical tools); no crate is
downloaded.

  unit matrices, number sets, groups and operation signs; the matrices
  and fields of the two spacetimes keep the tags 1671 and 1644 of the
  project.
\item
  \(D\) is the number of space dimensions in which the fields carry
  momentum: 7 on the eight-dimensional spacetimes, 3 in the
  four-dimensional world.
\item
  Units: \(\hbar\) and the speed of light are 1; the momentum cutoff
  \(\Lambda_{\rm NG}=1\) in the superfluid and in the Kaluza-Klein
  calculations.
\item
  No number is typed by hand into a document:
  \texttt{code/scripts/write\_doc\_tables.py} inserts them from the data
  files.
\end{itemize}
