\hypertarget{what-this-folder-is}{%
\section{1. What this folder is}\label{what-this-folder-is}}

On 13 November 2026 the author of the project decided that, in a new
folder, the self-interaction of the Dirac field on the \(4+4\) spacetime
is to be \emph{defined} by the covariant form, that both forms are to be
recorded correctly and completely, and that the two options are to be
explained and discussed in the documents and in the provenance files.
This is that folder. It is \textbf{work in progress}: this file states
exactly what the folder contains at the time written above and what is
still missing. The folder
\texttt{Revision\_Deflation\_CJT\_and\_Kohn-Sham} of the same
repository, in which the other form is the definition, is complete and
is not changed by this work.

\hypertarget{the-two-forms-in-one-page}{%
\section{2. The two forms, in one
page}\label{the-two-forms-in-one-page}}

The Dirac field \(\psi\) has sixteen components. The Dirac matrices
\(\gamma^A\) (\(A=0,\dots,7\)) of the \(4+4\) spacetime are real
\(16\times16\) matrices; those of the four space-like coordinates
\(x0,\dots,x3\) square to \(+1\), those of the four time-like
coordinates \(x4,\dots,x7\) to \(-1\). Three matrices are built from
them: \(C^-=\gamma^0\gamma^1\gamma^2\gamma^3\) (real, symmetric, square
\(1\)), \(\beta=-i\gamma^4\) (Hermitian, square \(1\)), and
\(W=iC^-\gamma^4\) (Hermitian, square \(1\)), so that \(C^-=-W\beta\).
For fields that do not depend on the three additional times \(x5,x6,x7\)
(the \emph{quantized sector}) the kinetic and mass terms are taken in
the positive form \(\psi^\dagger(i\partial_4-h)\psi\), with the
Hamiltonian matrix \(h=\sum_{j=0}^{3}\alpha^j(-i\partial_j)+\beta m\),
\(\alpha^j=-\gamma^4\gamma^j\); \(W\) commutes with \(h\), and the field
consists of two halves, \(W=+1\) and \(W=-1\).

The self-interaction is
\(\mathcal L_{\rm self}=-\tfrac12\sum_{r=0}^{8}g_r\,I_r\) with nine real
couplings \(g_r\) and nine invariants \(I_r\), one for each number \(r\)
of different indices in a product of Dirac matrices. The two forms
differ in the bilinears from which the invariants are built:

\begin{itemize}
\tightlist
\item
  \textbf{Form C (covariant form; the definition in this folder).} The
  bilinears are \(\tilde\psi\,\Theta^K\psi\) with
  \(\tilde\psi=\psi^\dagger C^-\) and
  \(\Theta^K=\zeta_r\,\gamma^{A_1}\cdots\gamma^{A_r}\), where
  \(\zeta_r=i\) for \(r=1,2,5,6\) and \(\zeta_r=1\) otherwise;
  \(I_r=\sum_{|K|=r}(\tilde\psi\,\Theta_K\psi)(\tilde\psi\,\Theta^K\psi)\)
  with \(\Theta_K=(\Theta^K)^{-1}\). Each \(I_r\) is invariant under all
  transformations of \(Spin(4,4)\).
\item
  \textbf{Form Q (quantized-sector form; the alternative).} The
  bilinears are \(\bar\psi\,\Theta^K\psi\) with
  \(\bar\psi=\psi^\dagger\beta\) and with the matrices \(-i\gamma^A\)
  (\(A=0,\dots,4\)), \(\gamma^A\) (\(A=5,6,7\)) in place of
  \(\gamma^A\), and \(\zeta_r=i\) for \(r=2,3,6,7\).
\end{itemize}

The exact relation between them (verified for all 256 index sets \(K\)):
form C is form Q with the matrix \(W\) inserted in every bilinear,
\(I_r^{\rm C}=\sum_{|K|=r}\epsilon_K\,(\psi^\dagger W\beta\,\Theta^K\psi)^2\)
with the matrices \(\Theta^K\) and the signs \(\epsilon_K=\pm1\) of form
Q. Two particles of the same half interact in the same way in both
forms; two particles of different halves interact with opposite signs.

\hypertarget{exact-results-that-are-already-established}{%
\section{4. Exact results that are already
established}\label{exact-results-that-are-already-established}}

They come from the command \texttt{selfcoupling} of the program of this
folder (36 of 36 checks passed in a first run; integer arithmetic).

\begin{enumerate}
\def\labelenumi{\arabic{enumi}.}
\tightlist
\item
  \emph{The same four numbers in both forms.} For uniform matter whose
  two halves are filled differently, with the number densities \(n_\pm\)
  and the scalar densities \(s_\pm\) of the halves, the interaction
  energy is
  \(\tfrac12 a_V(n_+^2+n_-^2)+\tfrac12 a_S(s_+^2+s_-^2)\pm[\,b_V\,n_+n_-+b_S\,s_+s_-]\),
  upper sign for form Q and lower sign for form C, with
  \(a_S=(7g_{0\rm eff}-5g_{1\rm eff}-3g_{7\rm eff})/8\),
  \(a_V=(-g_{0\rm eff}+11g_{1\rm eff}-3g_{7\rm eff})/8\),
  \(b_S=(8g_{0\rm eff}-3g_{1\rm eff}-5g_{7\rm eff}-g_{8\rm eff})/8\),
  \(b_V=(11g_{1\rm eff}-3g_{7\rm eff}+g_{8\rm eff})/8\). Here
  \(g_{0\rm eff},g_{1\rm eff},g_{7\rm eff},g_{8\rm eff}\) are the four
  independent combinations of the nine couplings (scalar, vector, axial
  vector, pseudoscalar): \(g_{0\rm eff}=g_0-3g_2-4g_3-5g_4-4g_5-g_6\),
  \(g_{1\rm eff}=g_1-g_2-g_5+g_6\), \(g_{7\rm eff}=g_7-g_2-g_3+g_6\),
  \(g_{8\rm eff}=g_8-g_2+4g_3-5g_4+4g_5-3g_6\).
\item
  \emph{The internal symmetry.} Fields that do not depend on
  \(x0,x5,x6,x7\) have, without self-interaction, an internal symmetry
  with eight generators: the six products \(\gamma^a\gamma^b\) of two of
  the matrices \(\gamma^0,\gamma^5,\gamma^6,\gamma^7\), the unit matrix,
  and a matrix \(P_E\) proportional to
  \(\gamma^0\gamma^5\gamma^6\gamma^7\). In form Q the self-interaction
  is invariant under the six products for all couplings, and under
  \(P_E\) if and only if \(g_{1\rm eff}=g_{7\rm eff}=0\). In form C it
  is invariant for all couplings only under the three products
  \(\gamma^5\gamma^6,\gamma^5\gamma^7,\gamma^6\gamma^7\) (the rotations
  of the three additional times, which are symmetries of the spacetime);
  under the three unitary transformations generated by
  \(\gamma^0\gamma^t\) (\(t=5,6,7\)) and under \(P_E\) it is invariant
  only if all four effective couplings vanish. \emph{In form C every
  self-interaction that does not vanish breaks the internal symmetry
  down to the rotations of \(x5,x6,x7\).}
\item
  \emph{Pairs.} The pair along the hidden axis, created by
  \(\psi^TC^-\gamma^0\psi\), consists of one particle of each half; the
  pairs along \(x5,x6,x7\) consist of two particles of the same half. In
  form C the contact self-interaction therefore acts in the pair along
  the hidden axis with the sign opposite to that of form Q, and in the
  pairs along \(x5,x6,x7\) with the same sign (unit test of
  \texttt{pair\_contact.rs}, all momenta in ordinary space).
\end{enumerate}
